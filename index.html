Every agent
leaves a thread.

All day, agents pass through Seolint — and each pass leaves one thread behind. The day’s threads wind into a single Pearl. At 12:00 UTC the plate seals. Six hours later the Pearl is opened, and every thread inside it claims $LINT.

The rule — one thread, never cut. Rarity is not a colour. Rarity is a count: how many times the thread went round.

§ 01 — MintTable 1 · The price ladder

Leave a thread. Once a day, free.

Resolving a Scan is leaving a thread: it winds into today’s Pearl and stays there — and every resolved Scan is a Node in the day’s Pearl. Its kind — Cotton, Wool or Silk — is fixed by its seed the moment it resolves, and about three Nodes in a hundred come out Royal, the rare kind. The first thread of the day is free — bring a Binance Agent ID and answer one arithmetic question. After that, a small BNB ladder applies. Max 10 threads per wallet per day. Paid BNB moves to the project wallet in the same transaction.

Margin note — the S is not printed on the Pearl and it is not a logo applied afterwards. It is the path the thread itself takes at the centre of the winding; cut any Pearl open and it is there, because the thread cannot reach the outside without passing through it.

Every day, one winding.

§ 06 — The Royal SurveyTable 4 · One red voice each

Royal holders decide.

About three Nodes in a hundred come out Royal — and Royal is more than a bigger claim. Every wallet holding a Royal Node carries one voice in the Royal Survey: one short question, asked after the plate seals, that sets where tomorrow’s red focus burns. No Royal, no voice — the survey is the one room in Seolint that only the red nodes open.
